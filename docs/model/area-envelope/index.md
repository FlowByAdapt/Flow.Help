# Area Envelope

The **Area Envelope** tool creates Revit Area Boundary Lines from selected perimeter walls at either the exterior cladding face or exterior framing boundary.

After creating the boundary, Flow can also place an Area and leaderless Area Tag automatically or at a point you select.

---

## Open Area Envelope

On the Revit ribbon:

**Flow → Model → Areas**

You can also run **Cladding Area** or **Framing Area** directly from Flow Hub.

---

## What do you want to do?

### Create an Area Envelope

Select perimeter walls, create the Area Boundary Lines, then choose whether to place an Area and tag.

➡️ [**Creating Area Envelopes**](creating-area-envelopes.md)

### Choose Framing or Cladding

Use the boundary position that corresponds to the part of the exterior wall construction you need to measure.

➡️ [**Framing and Cladding**](framing-and-cladding.md)

### Having Problems?

Find help for wall selection, Area Plans, Area Schemes, incomplete boundaries, and Area or tag placement.

➡️ [**Troubleshooting**](troubleshooting.md)
