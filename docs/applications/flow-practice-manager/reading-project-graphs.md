# Reading Project Graphs

## Check What the Graph Shows

Read the title, legend and axis labels. Check the unit, reporting period, and which projects or stages are included.

## Check the Figures Behind a Change

If a stage looks close to its hours budget, use the project review figures to check recorded hours and the forecast for the remaining work. For the sample alteration, 32 recorded hours against a 40-hour design budget leaves 8 hours in the budget. A 12-hour forecast for remaining work puts the stage 4 hours over budget.

Before comparing two views, check that they use the same period, stages and filters.
