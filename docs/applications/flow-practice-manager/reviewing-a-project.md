# Reviewing a Project

Use a project review to check how each active stage is tracking and decide what needs to happen before the next review.

---

## Review the Stage

1. Open the project and check that you’re reviewing the right stage.
2. Compare recorded hours with the stage’s hours budget.
3. Review the forecast of hours still needed to complete the agreed work.
4. Compare recorded hours plus forecast hours with the budget to see whether the stage is likely to run over.

!!! info "Allow for work still to do"

    On the sample alteration, the design stage has a 40-hour budget. The team has recorded 32 hours and expects to need another 12. The stage is forecast to finish 4 hours over budget, even though recorded hours are still below budget.

---

## Record the Next Action

If the forecast is over budget, check what work remains and whether it is part of the agreed scope. In the sample alteration, the project lead could check whether another design revision is included before the team starts it.

Record the next action, who’s responsible and when to check in again. At the next review, check the action and update the forecast for any work still to do.
