# Understanding Permissions

Permissions determine which records you can see and which actions you can take in a shared Practice Manager environment.

## Where Access Can Vary

| Area | Examples of access that may differ |
| --- | --- |
| Projects and sites | Viewing a project, editing its details or changing the linked site. |
| Timesheets and costs | Recording your own time or costs and viewing entries across the team. |
| Proposals and claims | Preparing a draft, reviewing or approving it, and accessing fees or billing details. |
| Reports and graphs | Viewing data for a project, a team or the whole practice. |

Your practice determines the access you need for your work. Commercial information such as internal rates or margins may have more restricted access.

## If an Action Is Unavailable

Check that you are in the right project and that the record is at the stage where the action applies. If you still can’t see or use the action, ask your practice administrator to check your access. Include the project reference, the action you tried and any message shown.

!!! note "Access can differ between environments"

    Your practice administrator can confirm the access assigned to you in your environment.
