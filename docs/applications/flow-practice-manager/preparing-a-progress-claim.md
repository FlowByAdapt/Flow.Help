# Preparing a Progress Claim

Use the project’s fee schedule and previous claims to check the amount to claim for the current period.

---

## Check the Fee Schedule

Open the project and review the fees by stage. Check that the schedule reflects the approved proposal or the fee agreement entered directly for the job.

For an existing project, check previously issued amounts by stage and the starting claim number before creating the first claim in Practice Manager.

If the earlier claim is in the synced project files, expand **Previous claims from project files** and select **Find previous payment claims**. Choose the verified issued claim, check its cumulative amounts against the issued document, and match the amounts to the fee stages. Confirm the issue before applying the opening balances, then select **Save fee schedule**.

---

## Review the Current Claim

Check the amount claimed to date against the total amount you intend to claim through the current period. The difference is the amount for this period.

For the sample alteration, the client has approved the design stage. The example uses fictional figures on the same tax basis.

| Design stage | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Agreed stage fee | $4,000 |
| Claimed in previous periods | $1,000 |
| Total to claim through this period | $2,500 |
| **Amount to claim this period** | **$1,500** |

Check that each stage on the claim is part of the approved scope and that previous claims have been accounted for.

---

## Reconcile the Project Financials

Open **Project financials** from the project to compare agreed or estimated stage fees, opening and issued claims, pending claims and invoice drafts. These are different stages of the same billing workflow. Use the issued and opening claim totals to check amounts previously claimed.

---

## Check the Invoice Draft

Review and save the claim with its **Document details**, then prepare the linked invoice draft. Check the client, dates and amounts against the reviewed claim before transferring the draft. Finalise and issue the invoice in Xero.

If the Xero draft exists and the claim PDF still needs attention, use **Check / upload PDF** on the existing transfer. It checks for the saved PDF and retries the attachment against that invoice. **Check Xero status** only checks the saved transfer and attachment status.
